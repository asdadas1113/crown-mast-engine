# Research results plan

## Status

The current numeric checkpoint documents (including prior 64-point / 1,024-point sweeps and stress checks) are primarily **engine-development, validation, and exploratory checkpoints**. They are useful for regression checks, hypothesis formation, and detecting structural problems, but they are not the canonical final research dataset.

## Future canonical results

When the engine/mechanics/sample roster is ready and the user explicitly starts the research-results phase, create a separate **Research Results** section and rerun the intended study batches with the then-current audited engine.

That results section will be the canonical place to record:

- exact engine/mechanics revision;
- roster and character implementation scope;
- fixed Crown/Mast assumptions;
- growth-grid definitions;
- boss conditions;
- rotation/opening definitions;
- all aggregate results and important per-case outputs;
- robustness/sensitivity checks;
- unresolved source/mechanics limitations.

Existing checkpoint documents may remain as development history, but they must not be silently promoted into final results.

## Current phase

Current work remains **engine/character validation and sample preparation**. Do not run or refresh full research-result batches unless the user explicitly says to begin the results phase. Small executions needed for tests, regression checks, or mechanic validation are allowed and should remain clearly separated from research-result collection.
